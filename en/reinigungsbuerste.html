Questions and answers

Is one battery enough for the whole bathroom?

Usually, yes. According to the manufacturers many models last 60 to 90 minutes, and testers got through a bathroom easily on one charge. They charge with a USB cable like a phone, which usually takes around three hours.

Can it get wet?

It can handle splashes. But you shouldn't hold it under water. You can take the heads off and rinse them.

Which head is for what?

Round brush for tiles and the tub, corner brush for grout and corners, sponge for washbasins and taps, flat brush for the floor.

Do I still need cleaning products?

Yes. The brush does the scrubbing for you, but not the cleaning product. Let it work for a few minutes, then brush. For dirty grout you need a strong cleaner.

Can it scratch surfaces?

Yes, that can happen. Hard bristles and scouring pads can scratch acrylic, glass-ceramic, stainless steel or paintwork. Use a soft sponge or a microfibre pad there and test it on a hidden spot first.
