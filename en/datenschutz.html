2. General

I take the protection of your data seriously. This website has no contact form and no user accounts. No cookies are set for advertising or analytics. For simple visitor statistics without cookies I use Cloudflare Web Analytics (see section 3). You can sign up for the newsletter voluntarily (see section 7).

No content from other providers is embedded on the website: no YouTube videos, no maps, no social media buttons and no ad banners. The explainer videos and pictures are all stored on this server. The source code of the home page also contains verification codes for Google Search Console and Pinterest. They only show that the website belongs to me. No data about you is sent to Google or Pinterest because of them.

I run this website from Switzerland. That's why the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) applies. Because the site is also aimed at readers in the EU, I also comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

3. Hosting, server log files and visitor statistics

This website is hosted by Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open the site, Cloudflare automatically processes technical data that your browser sends (for example IP address, date and time, the page requested, browser type). This is necessary to deliver the website securely and reliably. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a working service). I myself have no access to individual log files and cannot identify visitors from them.

There is a data processing agreement with Cloudflare under Art. 28 GDPR. Data may be transferred to the USA. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. For this framework the European Commission has found an adequate level of data protection (Art. 45 GDPR). For Switzerland the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework applies, for which the Swiss Federal Council has also found an adequate level of protection. More about this in Cloudflare's privacy policy.

For simple visitor statistics I use Cloudflare Web Analytics. For this, your browser loads a small script from Cloudflare (static.cloudflareinsights.com) when you open the site. It sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device and creates no profile of you. I only see aggregated numbers, for example how often a page was viewed, which page visitors came from, from which country and with which browser. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in improving the service).

4. Browser storage

When you click a link to another MYLE page, the website briefly stores a marker in your browser's session storage (sessionStorage): which page you are opening and when. This makes the new page start at the top instead of in the middle of the text. The marker is deleted as soon as the new page opens, at the latest when you close the tab. It is not sent to me or anyone else. This storage is technically necessary for the function you asked for and therefore needs no consent (Art. 5(3) ePrivacy Directive).

I switch off the visitor statistics for my own devices so that my visits do not distort the numbers. You can do this too: if you open the link myle.life/en/?nicht-zaehlen once, the website stores the entry “myle-nicht-zaehlen” in your browser’s storage (localStorage). As long as it is there, your browser no longer loads the Cloudflare statistics script. The entry contains only this one value and is not sent to me or anyone else. You remove it again with myle.life/en/?wieder-zaehlen or by deleting the website data in your browser. This storage is also necessary for the function you asked for and needs no consent (Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG, Art. 5(3) ePrivacy Directive). Without this link the website creates no lasting storage, and it uses no other cookies or storage.

7. Newsletter “New helpers by email”

If you sign up for the newsletter, your browser sends your email address and the chosen language to my mailing service Brevo when you click “Sign up”. Nothing is loaded from Brevo before that. The provider is Brevo GmbH, Köpenicker Str. 126, 10179 Berlin, Germany, a company of the Brevo group (Brevo SAS, Paris). There is a data processing agreement with Brevo (Art. 28 GDPR). Brevo stores the data on servers in the EU (Belgium, France and Germany). For Switzerland the EU counts as a region with an adequate level of data protection.

You then get an email with a confirmation link (double opt-in). Only when you click it is your address added to the mailing list for your language. As proof, Brevo stores when you signed up and when you confirmed. If you don't confirm, you won't get any more emails.

The newsletter comes from kontakt@myle.life, at most twice a month. Brevo counts how often the emails are opened and how often links in them are clicked. For this, every email contains a small invisible image, and links briefly pass through a Brevo server. I have switched on anonymous tracking: I only see totals, not whether you personally opened an email or clicked a link. If you don't want even that, turn off automatic image loading in your email program.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time, with the unsubscribe link in every email or by writing to kontakt@myle.life. After that you won't get the newsletter any more. Your address only stays on a block list at Brevo, so that you don't get emails again by mistake and I can prove the earlier consent (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If you want me to delete it completely, just write to me. More about this in Brevo's privacy policy.
