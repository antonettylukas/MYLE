3. Hosting, server log files and visitor statistics

This website is hosted by Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open the site, Cloudflare automatically processes technical data that your browser sends (for example IP address, date and time, the page requested, browser type). This is necessary to deliver the website securely and reliably. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a working service).

There is a data processing agreement with Cloudflare under Art. 28 GDPR. Data may be transferred to the USA. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. For this framework the European Commission has found an adequate level of data protection (Art. 45 GDPR). More about this in Cloudflare's privacy policy.

For simple visitor statistics I use Cloudflare Web Analytics. For this, your browser loads a small script from Cloudflare (static.cloudflareinsights.com) when you open the site. It sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device and creates no profile of you. I only see aggregated numbers, for example how often a page was viewed, which page visitors came from, from which country and with which browser. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in improving the service).
