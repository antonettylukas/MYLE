Questions and answers

Which e-reader is right for me?

For novels, the normal Kindle is all you need. If you read a lot, in bed in the evening or in the bath, take the Paperwhite: it has warm light and is waterproof. You only need colour for comics, children's books or pictures. And if you like borrowing from the library, a PocketBook is the better choice.

Can I borrow books from the library?

Not with the Kindle, unfortunately: the Onleihe service of German public libraries doesn't work on it. With PocketBook and Tolino you borrow right on the device, with Kobo via the computer.

Can I read my own files and PDFs?

Yes. With the Kindle you send files to the device with “Send to Kindle” via the app or email, including e-books without copy protection from other shops. With Kobo and PocketBook it works, for example, by cable from the computer.

Do I need Kindle Unlimited?

No. It's an optional subscription with a large selection of books. It's only worth it if you read a lot and like the selection. Best try it first, there's often a free trial month.

Are there ads on the device?

On the Kindle there can be. When buying, check whether it says “with ads”. That version shows offers on the lock screen and on the home screen and is a little cheaper.

How long does the battery last?

According to Amazon, a Kindle lasts 6 to 12 weeks depending on the model, if you read for about half an hour a day with Wi-Fi off. With a lot of light and Wi-Fi on, the battery runs out sooner.
