How to brush properly

With an electric toothbrush you brush differently than by hand:

  1. Put the brush in your mouth first, then switch it on, or it splashes
  2. Hold the head lightly against your teeth and move it slowly from tooth to tooth, don't scrub
  3. Little pressure, the brush does the work for you
  4. Done after two minutes, rinse the head briefly

Questions and answers

Does an electric toothbrush really clean better?

Yes. A large review of 51 studies (Cochrane, 2014) shows: people who brush electrically for more than three months have on average 21% less plaque and 11% less gum inflammation than with a manual toothbrush.

Sonic toothbrush or rotating head: which is better?

Both clean well. According to Stiftung Warentest, neither system has a clear advantage when brushing. In the latest test (issue 12/2025) a sonic toothbrush is ahead, the Philips Sonicare 5500 (grade 1.7). In the test before, an Oral-B with a round head won (grade 1.9). Take the one you like using.

How often do I change the brush head?

About every three months, or sooner if the bristles splay. Some models remind you.

How do I brush properly?

Twice a day for two minutes. You hold the head lightly against your teeth and move it slowly from tooth to tooth. You don't need to scrub, the brush does that.

Do I need the app?

No. On the Laifen Wave Pro the app lets you set how it brushes and shows you a report of your brushing times. You can brush without a phone at all.
