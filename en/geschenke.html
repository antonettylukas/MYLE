Questions and answers

What do you give someone who already has everything?

Ideally something that takes work off them every week. Most people have enough things, but hardly anyone has free time to spare. A robot vacuum and mop, an air fryer or a bean-to-cup coffee machine get used almost every day and remind them of the gift for a long time. Check carefully beforehand whether there's already one in the household.

Is a robot vacuum a good gift?

Yes, if there isn't too much lying around on the floor and the person is happy to set up an app once. With my calculator's starting values it saves around 50 hours of cleaning a year. Who it's not really for is explained honestly on the robot vacuum and mop page.

When do I need to order so it arrives before Christmas?

Unfortunately I can't promise you that. You can see how quickly a device will arrive directly on the product page at Amazon, where the estimated delivery date is shown. Large appliances often take longer than small ones. It's better to order one or two weeks early so you have some leeway.
