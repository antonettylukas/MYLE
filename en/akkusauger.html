Questions and answers

Will it replace my normal vacuum cleaner?

A good one does, especially on hard floors and carpets that aren't too thick. According to Stiftung Warentest, the best cordless vacuums keep up with classic vacuum cleaners. But not all of them are good: in the test in issue 2/2026, some models were only adequate or even poor.

How long does the battery last?

What manufacturers state usually applies to the lowest setting. On the highest setting it's often only 10 to 15 minutes. The battery of the Bosch Unlimited 10 can be swapped, so with a second one you can vacuum twice as long.

How do I empty the dust container?

Hold it over the bin and open the container, and the dust falls out. It gets a bit dusty. If you're allergic to house dust, it's best to empty it outside or let someone else do it.

Can it handle pet hair?

Not all equally well. At Stiftung Warentest, the Bosch Unlimited 10, the two Miele models and the Rowenta X-Force Flex 13.60 reliably removed pet hair, while others struggled. If long hair wraps around the brush, you cut it open and pull it off.

Are there any that mop at the same time?

Yes, so-called wet and dry vacuums, for example from Tineco or Dreame. They vacuum and mop at the same time and are worth it with lots of hard floors. But if you don't want to do anything yourself any more, take a look at the robot vacuum and mop. Since I've had one, I hardly ever vacuum myself.

How long does a vacuum like this last?

Usually the battery is the first thing to weaken, often after a few years. So make sure you can replace it and that replacement batteries are available. Bosch even gives a ten-year guarantee on the motor of the Unlimited 10.
