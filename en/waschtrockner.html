How to have the laundry done when you get home

Depending on the machine, the button is called “End time”, “Ready in” or “Delay start”. Here's how to use it:

  1. In the morning put the laundry in, no more than it can dry (usually 6 kg)
  2. Choose the “Wash and dry” programme
  3. Set the end time, for example 6 pm. It works out by itself when it has to start
  4. In the evening the laundry is dry. Take it out soon, then it stays nice and smooth

Questions and answers

A washer dryer, or a separate washing machine and dryer?

Two machines get more done at once: while the dryer is running, the washing machine is already washing the next load. But you need twice the space. A washer dryer fits where your washing machine is now, and you never have to move the wet laundry. That's exactly what makes it so convenient. If you wash several loads a day for a big family, two machines are the better choice.

How much laundry can it dry?

Usually about 6 kg, even if it can wash 9 or 10 kg. 6 kg is, for example, around 40 T-shirts. If you want to wash and dry in one go, only put in as much as it can dry.

Do I really not have to iron any more?

Mostly not for everyday clothes. A steam programme like i-⁠Refresh on my Haier freshens up clothes and smooths out creases. The only important thing is to take the laundry out soon after drying and fold it, then it stays smooth. You might still give a shirt for an important meeting a quick press.

Does the laundry then sit in the drum all day?

Yes, but dry. With the end time it starts so late that it finishes just when you get home. Until then the laundry sits dry in the drum. Wet laundry, on the other hand, shouldn't be left for hours, or it starts to smell.

What does drying cost?

Without a heat pump a drying cycle uses a good 2 to 2.5 kWh of electricity depending on the machine. That is around 80 cents at 37 cents per kWh. With three loads a week that comes to just over €10 a month. A machine with a heat pump like the AEG uses less electricity and water: Stiftung Warentest reckons it costs around €760 less to run over ten years than the Bosch and Siemens machines it tested.

What do I need to keep clean?

Now and then rinse out the lint filter at the bottom by the pump and the detergent drawer, and wipe the rubber seal on the door. A hot programme with an empty drum once a month keeps it fresh.

Does it need a special connection?

No. It needs the same water supply, the same drain and the same socket as a washing machine. So you simply put it in its place.
