Kitchen

Food processor with cooking function

✓ Last checked: November 2026

One appliance that chops, stirs and cooks at the same time, all in the same bowl. The best-known one is the Thermomix.

Soup, sauce, risotto or rice pudding: the machine stirs and heats at the same time, and you no longer have to stand at the stove. It chops onions and vegetables first in the same bowl, and with many models you weigh the ingredients right in it too. Afterwards you mainly wash the mixing bowl instead of a board, a knife and a pan.

Questions and answers

Why isn't the Thermomix included?

Vorwerk sells the Thermomix only itself, through its sales advisors and its own online shop. That's why it isn't on Amazon. It is good, though: at Stiftung Warentest the Thermomix TM7 was rated good (issue 2/2026). At Test-Achats the Moulinex Companion XL achieved a similar overall result.

And the Bosch Cookit, the test winner?

The Bosch Cookit was the test winner at Stiftung Warentest (good, grade 2.0, issue 1/2024). But it isn't available on Amazon, so it's missing here.

What about Lidl's Monsieur Cuisine?

Lidl sells the Monsieur Cuisine Smart itself. At Stiftung Warentest it was satisfactory (grade 2.8, issue 1/2024), and at Test-Achats it is a best value tip. If you can get it at Lidl, it's an affordable alternative with built-in scales.

Can I sear food in it?

Only to a limited extent. According to Stiftung Warentest, roasted flavours only develop from 140 degrees. The ClickChef only reaches 120 degrees, so nothing really browns. For steak, schnitzel or crispy onions, you're better off sticking with the frying pan.

Do onions turn to mush?

Easily, yes. At Stiftung Warentest almost all machines slipped up when chopping onions (issue 1/2024). It's best to chop onions with a few short presses of the pulse or turbo button and check in between.

Do I need a subscription for recipes?

Not for the Companion XL: the recipes are in the free Moulinex app. For the ClickChef they are in the cookbook, and according to the manufacturer the WMF comes with just over 300 recipes on the machine. With the Thermomix you need the Cookidoo subscription for most recipes; without it, according to Vorwerk, you have around 200 recipes on the machine.

How much time does it really save?

Honestly, no test has measured that. The machine doesn't necessarily cook faster. But it saves you standing next to the pan and stirring, and you don't have to chop onions and vegetables by hand. In that time you can set the table, tidy up or simply sit down. The calculator shows how much that adds up to for you.
