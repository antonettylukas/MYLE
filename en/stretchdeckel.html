Questions and answers

Do they really seal tightly?

Yes, if the lid is pulled fully over the rim and the rim is dry. In tests, not even water ran out of a tipped bowl. But they're not as airtight as a vacuum container, and I wouldn't carry a bowl of soup upside down with one.

Can they go in the microwave?

Most can, check the packaging. Leave a small gap open when heating so the steam can escape.

Which size do I take?

The lid should be a little smaller than the rim, then it stretches over it. A set with six sizes covers everything from half a lemon to a bowl just over 20 cm across. For bigger bowls you need a set with an extra-large lid.

How long do they last?

That depends on the quality. At America's Test Kitchen all the lids survived ten dishwasher cycles without damage; for other testers, some tore while being put on. Don't stretch them more than necessary and keep them away from knives and sharp edges.

Do they take on smells?

Yes, after onion, garlic or mustard they often smell for a while. It fades with every wash. If the smell lingers, rub them with a little baking soda and a damp sponge.
